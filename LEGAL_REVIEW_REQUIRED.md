# Legal review

Pages: Terms, Privacy, Cookie, Subscription Terms, Refund and Cancellation, Acceptable Use, DPA, Security (`src/lib/content/legal-documents.ts`).

Written for UK B2B SaaS: England and Wales law, liability cap of 12 months fees (UCTA reasonableness), Late Payment Act interest, UK GDPR Article 28 processor terms, ICO complaint route. These are drafts based on common practice, not legal advice.

Ask a solicitor to confirm: liability cap and exclusions, 30 day price change notice, refund position, sub-processor list (name Mollie, hosting, email, storage once chosen), international transfer wording, and whether a VAT line is needed.
